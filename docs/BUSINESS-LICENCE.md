Version 1, published 5 October 2026. It applies to orders placed from the day the licence goes on sale; until then
nothing is sold under it.

## 1. What this is

Anode is open-source software under the MIT licence (the `LICENSE` file in the Anode repository). This Business licence
**does not replace or restrict the MIT licence**, and nobody needs it to use Anode, at work or anywhere else. It is a paid
licence and support agreement for organisations that want a written record of a paid licence and a priority lane for
their issues.

  A separate order has its own term.

## 3. What the licensee gets

During the term:

1. **A licence record and an invoice.** This document (version 1, delivered as a file with the order and kept at a fixed
   address), the Gumroad receipt, which records the number of seats and the purchase date, and the invoice Gumroad
   generates for the order (from the "Generate" link on the receipt, where a business adds its name, address and VAT
   number). The licensee may show them to its procurement, legal or compliance teams as evidence of the paid agreement.
   The licence key on the receipt only identifies the order: Anode never asks for it.
2. **Priority triage.** GitHub issues in `skulitom/Anode` whose title starts with `[business]` are looked at before other
   issues once the licensee is confirmed. To confirm, either name the licensee in the issue, or, to keep its name out of
   a public issue, email the notices address from the order's email address with a link to the issue. Unconfirmed
   `[business]` issues are handled as ordinary issues. Never post a licence key in an issue: issues are public. This is
   best effort: **no service level**, no promised response time, and no promise to build any feature or fix any bug.

Nothing else is promised. In particular, nothing here entitles the licensee to any future build or edition of Anode.

- It isn't a permission the licensee needs: the MIT licence already allows commercial use.

- It gives no warranty beyond the MIT licence's, and no consulting, development or security-audit services.

## 5. Price, renewal and changes

The price is the price on the Gumroad page at purchase, per seat per year, plus any VAT or sales tax Gumroad adds at
checkout. The licence renews automatically each year, and Gumroad charges the payment method on file on each renewal
date, until the licensee cancels from the "Manage membership" link on the receipt. The licensor may change these terms
or the price for a renewal by posting the new terms at the URL below, and emailing the licensee through Gumroad, at
least 30 days before that renewal; the licensee may cancel before it renews. The terms and price that applied when the
licensee bought or last renewed stay in force until then.

## 6. Refunds and cancellation

- **Refunds:** the licensee may ask for a full refund of a purchase or renewal within 30 days of it, for any reason, by
  writing to the notices address or opening an issue titled "licensing" (without the licence key). The licensor makes the
  refund through Gumroad. When a refund is made, the licence record and the triage lane for that period end. The MIT
  rights in the code are never affected.
- **Cancellation:** the licensee may cancel at any time from the "Manage membership" link on the Gumroad receipt. The
  licence then ends at the end of the paid term. Refunds outside the 30 days are at the licensor's discretion.

## 7. Ending early

Either side may end this agreement if the other materially breaks it and doesn't fix the breach within 30 days of
written notice (an email to the notices address, or to the email on the Gumroad order). The service continues during
those 30 days. If the licensor's breach isn't fixed, the licensee gets a refund for the unused part of the term. If the
licensee's breach isn't fixed, the licensor may end the triage lane, without a refund. The MIT rights in the code are
never affected.
